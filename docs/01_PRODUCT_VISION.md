# 01 — Product Vision

## Working category

Live classroom canvas companion.

## Users

### Teacher
Needs to teach normally, not operate a complicated classroom control center. The product must disappear behind the act of teaching.

### Student
Needs access to the same board without interrupting the teacher, and a low-social-risk way to express confusion.

## Product principles

1. **Teacher flow comes first.** XP-Pen writing must feel immediate.
2. **Students have camera independence.** Looking back must never affect anybody else.
3. **Follow is reversible.** One tap follows; manual navigation breaks follow; one tap returns.
4. **Anonymous to humans, accountable to systems.** Teacher does not need identity; system needs pseudonymous abuse control.
5. **Questions, not chat.** The teacher sees useful doubts, not a conversation feed.
6. **AI removes noise; it does not control learning.** Legitimate but unusual questions should fail open to a low-priority queue rather than disappear.
7. **Every class leaves an artifact.** Saved board first; AI summary second.

## Differentiation

The differentiator is not whiteboard drawing. It is combining independent student navigation, teacher-follow mode, anonymous structured doubt collection, and post-class preservation in one classroom workflow.

## Success signal

The teacher can finish a class without changing how they teach, while students report fewer missed board moments and more willingness to ask doubts.
